# Benchmark 1 — PLUTO-to-PLUTO Conducted Test

**Status: NOT STARTED — Future work**
Blocked pending acquisition of verified RF attenuators. Connecting two PLUTOs
directly via coax without attenuation risks driving the RX input above its safe
limit (~+2.5 dBm) and may damage the hardware. This benchmark cannot be safely
executed until calibrated attenuators (≥ 30 dB) are available.

See `docs/test_logs/decisions.md` Decision 13 for the rationale.
